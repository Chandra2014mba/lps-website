The 2025 GST Council meeting introduced significant changes that affect businesses across Bangalore. This guide breaks down what changed and how it impacts your compliance.

## Key Changes

Insurance premiums are now exempt from GST, rate slabs have been simplified for MSMEs, and new e-invoicing rules apply to businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore.
